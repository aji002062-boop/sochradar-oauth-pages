Privacy Policy

SochRadar Drive Backup is a personal automation application operated by its owner.

It requests Google Drive access to create, upload and manage generated video files, captions and related metadata.

Google OAuth credentials are used to authorize access. Automation credentials are stored in private configuration and GitHub Actions secrets.

Generated content may be processed using connected AI services and uploaded to Instagram when publishing is enabled.

The owner can revoke Google Drive access through Google Account security settings.

The application is not intended to provide access to other people's Drive accounts.
